# Historical data, execution and validation

CSV requires `timestamp,open,high,low,close,volume`. Timestamps must include offsets and are normalized to UTC. Validation rejects nonfinite/nonpositive prices, negative volume, impossible OHLC, duplicates, reversed ordering and cadence incompatible with the declared timeframe. Missing intervals are reported separately because weekends and holidays also produce gaps. No exchange calendar is inferred and no missing price is fabricated. Open-time CSVs must be converted to close/availability timestamps before import.

## Deterministic OHLC execution

Strategies see only candles through their decision close. OHLC simulation supports explicit midpoint and BID inputs. With BID input, a fixed assumed spread reconstructs ASK execution for long entries and short exits; long exits and short entries use BID. Dukascopy pipelines label BID provenance explicitly, and the simulator rejects mismatched or unknown bases. The spread is assumed constant, not measured from historical ASK quotes. Generic CSVs without an explicit basis retain the legacy midpoint assumption. A MARKET order fills at the next candle open using its side-aware quote plus adverse slippage. LIMIT and STOP orders wait for a qualifying future candle and expire after the signal's configured number of bars. They also support cancellation and rejection. Signal geometry, risk, position capacity, daily budget and costs are rechecked at the fill price. Invalid gaps reject an entry. No zero-sized trade is appended.

Stops and targets are mid-price levels. A bar touching both resolves stop-first. A gap through a stop uses the worse open. A target gets its specified level without favorable gap improvement. Exits also pay adverse half-spread and slippage. Commission is round-trip per unit: half charged on entry and half on exit. For a pending LIMIT/STOP entered intrabar, a same-bar target is deferred because it might have occurred before entry; a same-bar stop still applies conservatively. Intrabar entry sizing uses opening portfolio information; it does not infer an unobserved tick path.

`ExecutionAssumptions` configures warmup (default decision index 30), maximum positions (default 1), and optional UTC day-end liquidation (default false). Multiple positions share independent risk controls. Pending capacity is rechecked as each order fills. Open exposure is marked at opening gaps before new entry risk approval and at every candle close for equity/drawdown. End-of-data positions are liquidated for backtest reports; paper replay retains and checkpoints open positions. Day-start equity resets on UTC date changes. Optional day-end detection uses the configured interval, not a future bar's timestamp.

`TradeResult.pnl_per_unit` always means net P&L per unit; `TradeResult.pnl` multiplies by size exactly once. `equity_curve` is the realized trade-close curve retained for compatibility; `marked_equity_curve` includes open P&L each bar. Report drawdown uses marked equity. `net_pnl` is account-currency net P&L; `total_return_pct` is percentage return. The legacy `metrics()['total_return']` is an absolute-P&L alias. Profit factor without losses is infinite in Python and serialized as JSON null. Risk-adjusted performance is mean trade P&L divided by sample standard deviation, not an annualized Sharpe ratio. Consistency measures profitable chronological quartiles.

## Fair evaluation and robustness

Arena strategies receive identical candles, costs, capital and execution assumptions, with independent fresh strategy state. Session/regime attribution uses entry-decision information. Sessions are approximate UTC buckets without daylight-saving/calendar adjustment. Reports identify pair/timeframe; compare additional instruments by evaluating their independently supplied datasets.

Chronological holdout and rolling walk-forward preserve ordering. Training bars provide indicator warmup to test runs without contributing test P&L. An optional walk-forward `fit(train)` callback receives only training candles. The test starts flat and uses the last training close for the first test-open decision. Each fold has its own account; fold metrics are separate samples, not a continuous deployable equity curve.

Robustness perturbs spread, commission and slippage on training data and evaluates an untouched chronological holdout. Parameter sensitivity, if supplied, also runs only on training data. A ROBUST label requires adequate training and holdout trade samples, positive stressed/holdout expectancy, tolerable drawdown and cost sensitivity. Otherwise it reports FRAGILE or INSUFFICIENT_DATA. This diagnostic is heuristic and does not demonstrate a future edge. Default nonzero stress floors are proportional to training price when supplied costs are zero.

## Explicit limitations

There is no tick-level path, queue priority, partial filling, latency distribution, margin/leverage model, financing/swap, liquidity constraint, historical variable spread or automatic currency conversion. Gaps can exceed reserved stop risk and the 5% daily threshold; protections prevent additional approved risk but cannot cap a discontinuous price path. Actual venue execution is outside this stage. The 2026-10-11 EURUSD H4 evaluation is a bounded historical experiment with fixed assumed costs; its holdout and walk-forward results are weak/negative and do not establish profitability.
