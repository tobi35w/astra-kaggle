# Learning and model lifecycle

ML is disabled by default. Four currency pairs now have 22 quality-checked, segmented historical windows, with M1 and derived M5/M15/H1/H4 files. These datasets have not yet been used for ML training or evaluated across all pairs. Enabling a flag alone does not train a model. `ml.train` additionally requires caller-confirmed `MarketData.metadata['real_data'] is True`, adequate labeled observations (300 by default, with a minimum floor of 100), and both outcome classes. CSV import alone does not assert authenticity. See [four-pair data curation](historical-data-curation.md).

Performance memory now records source-separated BACKTEST and OOS trade outcomes in risk multiples (R), and PAPER trade outcomes in account currency. Each record carries its dataset version, experiment or paper portfolio identity, outcome availability time, and a drawdown unit matching its metric. Arena evaluations use the granular backtest experiment recorder instead of writing a second aggregate record in incompatible units. Before strategy selection, comparable evidence chooses one experiment stream for each strategy, pair, timeframe, regime, and session, preferring PAPER, then OOS, BACKTEST, and legacy evidence. It never pools results across sources, units, or reruns. This is an evidence-selection rule, not proof of predictive value; paper results remain a finite offline replay, and the local raw archive still has missing periods.

Supabase Journal persistence passed an append/idempotency/read-back verification earlier on 2026-10-11 and again at 19:01 UTC after an intervening timeout. Acquisition/import content versions distinguish changed datasets; the current EURUSD campaign records its strategy, costs, dataset and code version in a reproducible research audit. No production model was created.

The optional scikit-learn path computes causal one/five-bar returns, relative ATR and candle body fraction. Labels become available only at their forward horizon. Chronological validation purges training rows whose labels overlap the test boundary. The scaler fits only training rows. Logistic regression uses fixed deterministic settings; validation reports accuracy and Brier score. No shuffled cross-validation or automatic hyperparameter search is used.

Models store a content version, feature schema, training/validation boundaries, availability time, scaler parameters, coefficients and validation metrics. Persistence uses JSON, not executable pickle files. Inference rejects unavailable versions and invalid feature shapes. Model estimates are recorded as supporting context when an available model is explicitly required; they do not bypass strategy or risk checks. Online monitoring compares realized Brier score with the model's validation baseline and persists calibration records. Sample quality, drift, calibration and economic utility still require empirical evaluation.

The real-data campaign under `data/astra-research-paper-20261011/` is a bounded empirical evaluation, not ML training. On EURUSD H4 from February 2022 through November 2023, the EMA-trend backtest took 25 trades and returned +0.70% under fixed assumed costs. The independent chronological holdout took 13 trades with -2.10% net return and -16.12 account-currency expectancy. Six walk-forward tests produced 34 trades total in separate fold accounts; one fold was positive and five were negative. Robustness is `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`. A paper replay ran 25 simulated trades; because its capped size is one instrument unit, its near-flat account-currency P&L is not comparable to the backtest's risk-sized account. These samples do not justify promoting the strategy. Tests exercise model training only on labeled synthetic fixtures explicitly marked for testing; those temporary models are not production artifacts or trading evidence.
